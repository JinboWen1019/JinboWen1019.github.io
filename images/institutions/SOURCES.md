# Institution Images

These small local WebP images identify the institutions in the Education and
Research Experience sections. Official marks are owned by their institutions.
No watermarked preview images are used.

- `cityu.webp`: City University of Hong Kong's official brand symbol, extracted
  from the vertical logo linked by its Department of Computer Science.
  Source: https://template.cityu.edu.hk/template/logo/CityU/CityU_Vertical_Logo_CMYK.svg
- `nuaa.webp`: Nanjing University of Aeronautics and Astronautics' official emblem.
  Source page: https://www.nuaa.edu.cn/2017/0116/c589a18507/page.htm
  Image: https://www.nuaa.edu.cn/_upload/article/images/23/c5/fe0e593e4be58505b05bc1940aff/cd8aa9a1-72ab-4830-a115-7d653d333b0e.jpg
- `gdut.webp`: Guangdong University of Technology's official emblem, extracted
  from the original transparent vertical-logo download, not the watermarked preview.
  Source page: https://photo.gdut.edu.cn/info/1324/1992.htm
  Original download: https://photo.gdut.edu.cn/system/_content/download.jsp?urltype=news.DownloadAttachUrl&owner=1266070172&wbfileid=2447EBBA0A00ED8EF5FF779C527EA97A
- `metaxlab.webp`: The MetaX image supplied by the site owner in this conversation.
  Resized without adding a watermark; displayed with a circular CSS mask.
