Hello, I’m Jinbo.

About Me

I will join the Department of Computer Science at City University of Hong Kong as a Ph.D. student in September 2026, advised by Prof. Man Hon Cheung. I received my M.Eng. in Computer Science and Technology from Nanjing University of Aeronautics and Astronautics, advised by Prof. Yang Zhang, and my B.Eng. in Data Science and Big Data Technology from Guangdong University of Technology.

My research explores how generative models, optimization, and incentive mechanisms can make next-generation wireless and networked systems more intelligent, efficient, and trustworthy. Current topics include LLM agents for network optimization, generative diffusion models for mechanism design, digital-twin migration, and blockchain networking.
