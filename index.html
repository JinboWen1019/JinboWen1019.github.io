About Me

I am a first-year Ph.D. student in the Department of Computer Science at City University of Hong Kong, advised by Prof. Man Hon Cheung. I received my M.Eng. in Computer Science and Technology from Nanjing University of Aeronautics and Astronautics, advised by Prof. Yang Zhang, and my B.Eng. in Data Science and Big Data Technology from Guangdong University of Technology, where I was advised by Prof. Jiawen Kang.

My research explores how generative AI, optimization, and incentive mechanisms can make next-generation wireless and networked systems more intelligent, efficient, and trustworthy.

  • LLM agents for network optimization ☘️
  • Generative AI for mechanism design ☘️
  • Digital twin migration ☘️
  • Blockchain networking ☘️

If you have any questions or are interested in collaborating with me, please feel free to contact me (E-mail: jinbo1608@163.com or WeChat: leslie_13682411608)😊.
